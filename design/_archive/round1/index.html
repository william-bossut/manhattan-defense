MANHATTAN DEFENSE

Four visual directions for "The Ascent" — same palette (#D81F26 on black/grey), same tiny playable ship, same altitude slider. Open each, drag the slider or hit Play, then decide which direction (or mix) to build for real. Nothing here is final game code — see ../docs/ for the full write-up.

A. Red Noir

Pure black, dense filled towers, red-lit windows only, strongest scanlines. Most faithful to "only red exists."

FilledHigh contrastScanlines

B. Night Glow

Dark navy night sky, soft red bloom and fog for depth. Most classically "cyberpunk atmosphere."

FilledBloomFog

C. Dot-Matrix HUD

Same pixel game world as A, but menus/HUD borrow Open Island & OpenBand's literal dot-grid, pills and flat, no-glow chrome.

FilledDot-gridOn-brand chrome

D. Wireframe Noir

New: monochrome white line-art architecture on black, hazard hatch-marks and hanging chains — after the Visiophobia reference. Red drops to a rare accent.

Outline onlyReference-drivenRed = rare

Controls in each mockup: Arrows/WASD to move, Space to shoot, drag the altitude slider to preview street → orbit, or check "auto-climb" to watch it happen on its own.
